About Us and Our Project

We are Madison West Rocket Club, and this is project TREMOR! Project TREMOR’s payload is designed to test the vibration and movement reduction capabilities of TPU filament with differing infill patterns. To detect movements in each of our four chambers, we will be using accelerometers at the center of each chamber, with a control accelerometer mounted in the avionics bay. The gathered information will then be analyzed and compared with the control and with each other. Data we gather could aid in future vibration-reducing materials used in rocketry.
